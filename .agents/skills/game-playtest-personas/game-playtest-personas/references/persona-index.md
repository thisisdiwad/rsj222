# Persona index — 20 fictional analysis lenses

This roster is a hand-authored coverage matrix, not a representative sample and not a demographic model.
It varies explicit factors such as platform, experience, motivations, patience, play pattern, spending stance,
and stated constraints. Names, ages, and genders provide fictional context; never use them alone to infer
behavior. Motivation labels borrow vocabulary from Quantic Foundry's model, but the roster has not been
validated as a predictor of real players. See [evidence and limitations](evidence-and-limitations.md).

## Recommended panels (subsets for focused questions)

Use the full 20 for a broad review. For a specific decision, these subsets concentrate the right eyes:

- **Onboarding / first 10 minutes:** Zoe (01), Barbara (08), Grace (03), Diego (04), George (20), Nadia (12).
  *Why:* explicit newcomer, low-patience, and legibility constraints can flag onboarding questions early.
- **Monetization / IAP / gacha / ads:** Lucia (10), Ahmed (16), Nadia (12), Grace (03), Priya (05).
  *Why:* a spender, a purist, and casuals expose both revenue upside and backlash.
- **Difficulty & combat tuning:** Marcus (02), Kenji (07), Emma (17), George (20), Hana (19), Diego (04).
  *Why:* pairs difficulty-seekers against difficulty-averse and low-skill players.
- **Bug hunt / stability / edge cases:** Viktor (18), Alex (06), Sam (11), Hana (19), Kenji (07).
  *Why:* the tinkerers, breakers, and completionists who go where others don't.
- **Accessibility lead sweep:** Barbara (08), George (20), Grace (03), Tom (09).
  *Why:* their explicit constraints can flag some legibility, complexity, timing, and input questions. This
  subset does not cover hearing, color vision, screen readers, photosensitivity, vestibular needs, or the
  range of cognitive and motor disabilities; use [the accessibility boundary](accessibility-review.md).
- **Endgame / retention / progression:** Sam (11), Marcus (02), Robert (13), Lucia (10), Tom (09).
  *Why:* the players who reach and judge the long tail.
- **Social / co-op / community features:** Priya (05), Chris (15), Nadia (12), George (20), Kenji (07).
  *Why:* covers cooperative, competitive, family, and creator-social angles.
- **Narrative / story / world:** Emma (17), Tom (09), Zoe (01), Chris (15), Robert (13).
  *Why:* immersion- and story-motivated players judge pacing and payoff.

## Selection guidance

- If unsure, choose the smallest panel that covers the decision; run all 20 only for a deliberately broad sweep.
- Include contrasting explicit constraints when relevant, but never require a persona to dislike the artifact.
- Match the panel to the platform when possible: don't over-weight PC hardcore for a mobile casual game.
- For children, disabled players, culturally specific audiences, experts, or serious-game domains, do not
  treat this generic roster as domain evidence. Recruit the relevant real participants and specialists.
