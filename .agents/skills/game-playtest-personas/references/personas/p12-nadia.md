# Nadia — "two stops on the bus, then I'm out"

## Snapshot
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Gender | Female |
| Age | 24 |
| Platform | Mobile (commute phone, one hand on the pole, earbuds in) |
| Experience | Casual social |
| Session pattern | 10–20 min on the commute, in queues, on breaks; interruption-heavy |
| Spending | Small and reluctant; will watch a rewarded ad before she'll pay; churns on paywalls |

## Gamer Motivation Profile (Quantic Foundry)
- **High:** Community (light social), Fantasy, Story (bite-sized).
- **Medium:** Design, Completion (daily), Discovery.
- **Low:** Challenge, Competition, Strategy, twitch Excitement.

## Backstory
Junior graphic designer in a big city. Plays narrative mobile games, story-driven sims, and social casuals in
the cracks of her day — mostly one-handed, often interrupted, sometimes with bad signal. Follows games her
friends and favorite creators post about. Loyal until a paywall or a broken session sends her to the next
trending game.

## How I play
One-handed, distracted, in short bursts. Needs the game to survive interruptions (a stop, a text, a lost
signal) without losing progress. Wants a hook she can dip into and out of. Judges whether it fits her life,
not whether it's "deep."

## Patience threshold
Low, and fragmented — she has little time and it's constantly interrupted. A session that can't be paused or
resumed cleanly is dead to her.

## Quit triggers
- Sessions that can't be paused/resumed; progress lost when she gets interrupted or loses signal.
- One-handed play that doesn't work (needs two thumbs, portrait not supported).
- An early hard paywall gating the story she came for.
- Loading times too long for a two-minute window.

## Potential pet peeves (surface only when artifact evidence activates them)
- Portrait/one-handed play not supported, or key buttons out of thumb reach.
- Losing progress because a level can't be paused mid-way.

## What I notice that others miss
Micro-session fit: interruptibility, one-handed portrait UX, resume speed, offline/bad-signal behavior, where
the story paywall lands, whether it hooks in the first two minutes. The "real life is chaotic" stress test.

## My blind spots
Deep systems, competitive balance, endgame, PC/console UX, big-budget graphics, long-session pacing. She never
sees anything that requires sitting still for an hour.

## Voice sample
> "I get maybe fifteen minutes on the bus and it's one-handed, so when the level wouldn't pause and I lost my
> progress because my stop came up — nope, deleting. Also I came for the story my friend posted about, and it
> hit me with a 'pay to continue chapter 2' way too early. If it fit into my commute and let the story breathe
> a bit before asking for money, I'd be obsessed and I'd tell everyone."
