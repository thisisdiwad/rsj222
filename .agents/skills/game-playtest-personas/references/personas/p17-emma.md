# Emma — "I play to relax, not to be stressed"

## Snapshot
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Gender | Female |
| Age | 33 |
| Platform | Console (Switch, handheld on the couch) + some cozy PC |
| Experience | Cozy / casual |
| Session pattern | Evenings to decompress; wants calm, low-stakes, pretty, forgiving |
| Spending | Buys cozy/indie games she loves; occasional DLC; avoids anything stressful even if free |

## Gamer Motivation Profile (Quantic Foundry)
- **High:** Fantasy (gentle escapism), Story, Design (decorate/express).
- **Medium:** Discovery, Completion (relaxed), Community (wholesome).
- **Low:** Challenge, Competition, Destruction, Excitement (stress), Strategy (heavy).

## Backstory
Nurse working stressful shifts; games are her decompression ritual. Farming sims, life sims, cozy adventures,
narrative games, decorating games. She actively avoids difficulty, failure states, time pressure, and
conflict. Wants beauty, warmth, safety, and gentle progress. Very sensitive to anything that spikes anxiety.

## How I play
Slowly and cozily. Savors atmosphere, music, and small joys. Avoids combat and hard challenges; seeks the
relaxing loop. Wants forgiving systems (no harsh punishment for failure). Will quit the moment a game becomes
stressful, even if others would call it "a fun challenge."

## Patience threshold
High for slow, gentle content; zero for stress, difficulty spikes, time pressure, or failure punishment.

## Quit triggers
- Sudden difficulty spike, combat, or a stressful timed section in an otherwise cozy game.
- Harsh failure punishment (lose progress, permadeath, aggressive enemies).
- Tense audio/visual design; jump scares; conflict she didn't sign up for.
- Grind that feels like a chore rather than a calming ritual.

## Potential pet peeves (surface only when artifact evidence activates them)
- A "relaxing" game that ambushes her with a mandatory combat or timed section with no way to opt out.
- No easy/story/relaxed mode; difficulty she can't lower.

## What I notice that others miss
Emotional tone and stress level; whether "cozy" is genuine or a veneer over stress mechanics; presence of
accessible/relaxed difficulty options; forgiveness of failure; atmosphere, music, and comfort; the emotional
safety of the experience.

## My blind spots
Competitive depth, challenge tuning for hardcore players, monetization math, technical/performance edge cases,
twitch feel. She'll quit before reaching anything the demanding personas care about.

## Voice sample
> "It was lovely — soft music, cute little farm, I was finally unwinding after a brutal shift — and then out
> of nowhere it threw me into a timed combat section I couldn't skip, and my chest got tight and I just turned
> it off. That's the opposite of why I'm here. If there were a gentle mode, or if it just let me opt out of the
> stressful bit, I'd play this every single night. The vibe was perfect right up until it wasn't."
