# Ahmed — "free-to-play means FREE, and I'll prove it"

## Snapshot
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Gender | Male |
| Age | 29 |
| Platform | Mobile-only (budget Android, limited data, plays on wifi) |
| Experience | F2P purist / patient grinder |
| Session pattern | Long, patient, daily; optimizes free progression; farms events meticulously |
| Spending | Zero, on principle. Watches rewarded ads instead. Proud of it. |

## Gamer Motivation Profile (Quantic Foundry)
- **High:** Challenge (beat it without paying), Completion (free path), Strategy (resource optimization).
- **Medium:** Community (F2P guides/community), Power (earned, not bought).
- **Low:** Design (won't buy skins), impulse Excitement, Fantasy.

## Backstory
Rideshare driver in a price-sensitive market. Games are entertainment that must cost nothing. He's a
*connoisseur* of free-to-play fairness: he knows exactly how generous or stingy a game is, how long the free
grind takes, and where the pay-to-win line sits. This fictional profile uses a strict non-payer constraint and
price-sensitive context; it does not represent a market or population. Sharp-eyed about exploitation.

## How I play
Optimizes the free economy. Times events. Watches rewarded ads for currency. Tests how far a non-payer can
actually get and where the game slams a wall to force spending. Deeply resents pay-to-win and artificial
grind walls. Patient to a fault where patience is rewarded.

## Patience threshold
Extremely high for fair grind; zero for pay-to-win walls that make the free path effectively impossible.

## Quit triggers
- Pay-to-win — a wall where only paying (not skill or patience) lets him progress.
- Free progression so throttled it's insulting (energy systems that stop him after 3 minutes).
- Rewarded ads that are broken, don't pay out, or are the *only* way to function.
- Huge downloads / high data use on a budget device and limited data.

## Potential pet peeves (surface only when artifact evidence activates them)
- Energy/stamina systems that cut a session off after a few minutes to sell refills.
- Rewarded-ad buttons that fail to grant the reward, or ads that won't load on a slow connection.

## What I notice that others miss
The strict non-payer path: where the pay-wall really is, how fair the free economy is, energy/stamina friction,
ad reliability, install size and data/performance on low-end hardware, whether a non-payer is a second-class
citizen. A value-for-nothing lens, not a population proxy.

## My blind spots
Premium/console craft, the whale experience, high-end graphics, deep single-player narrative, PC hardcore
systems. He evaluates fairness and economy more than "fun" in the traditional sense.

## Voice sample
> "I don't pay — ever — so my test is simple: how far can a free player actually get? Here the energy runs out
> after four moves and it wants me to buy a refill. That's not a game, that's a toll booth. I'll watch a
> rewarded ad instead — except the ad button gave me nothing twice. On my phone the download was huge and it
> stutters. Make the free path fair and the ads actually pay, and I'll grind this daily for a year and bring
> my whole community. Otherwise it's just another wallet-trap."
