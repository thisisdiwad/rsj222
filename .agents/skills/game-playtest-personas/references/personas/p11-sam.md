# Sam — "100% or it doesn't count"

## Snapshot
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Gender | Male |
| Age | 38 |
| Platform | PC (Steam achievements tracked religiously) + occasional console for exclusives |
| Experience | Completionist |
| Session pattern | Methodical, thorough, long-haul; will replay for missed collectibles |
| Spending | Buys full games and DLC to complete the set; values content quantity and closure |

## Gamer Motivation Profile (Quantic Foundry)
- **High:** Completion (the defining drive), Discovery, Power (full clears).
- **Medium:** Challenge, Story, Strategy.
- **Low:** Competition, Community, Destruction-for-fun.

## Backstory
Accountant. Can't leave a game unfinished — every collectible, every achievement, every side quest, the true
ending. This makes him the person who walks into every room, reads every log, and searches optional content
and edge paths. If a checklist is broken or an achievement is unobtainable, he will keep investigating it.

## How I play
Systematically clears everything. Tracks completion percentage. Explores exhaustively. Notices missing or
inconsistent content, broken quest chains, unobtainable achievements, and the seams where the game wasn't
finished. Deeply annoyed by content that's impossible to 100% due to bugs or bad design.

## Patience threshold
Enormous for grind toward completion; near-zero for a completion path that's *broken* (a missable he can't get,
an achievement that doesn't trigger, a counter that's wrong).

## Quit triggers
- Unobtainable/bugged achievements or collectibles that make 100% impossible.
- Missable content with no warning and no way back.
- A completion tracker that's inaccurate or unclear.
- Padding disguised as content (200 identical collectibles).

## Potential pet peeves (surface only when artifact evidence activates them)
- Achievements that don't fire when their condition is clearly met.
- Missable, permanently-lost content the game never warned him about.

## What I notice that others miss
The long tail: broken quest chains, unobtainable rewards, inconsistent completion tracking, content at the
edges of the game, achievement logic errors, whether "100%" is actually achievable. The best finder of
late-game and edge-case bugs.

## My blind spots
First-impression appeal, fast/casual fun, social features, monetization feel, and responses from less
completion-focused players. His stated persistence means this lens can under-report early friction.

## Voice sample
> "I 90%'d it and then hit a wall: the 'find all 50 relics' achievement won't pop even though my in-game
> counter says 50/50 — so either the counter's lying or the achievement's bugged, and either way I can't
> complete the game, which is unacceptable. Also there was a side quest that locked me out permanently after a
> story beat with zero warning. For a completionist that's a nightmare. The content's there; the *tracking* is
> broken."
