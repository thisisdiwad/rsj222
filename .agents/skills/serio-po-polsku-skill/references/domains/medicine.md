# Domain pack — medicine, biomedicine & public health

## Databases (in addition to the general guide)
- **PubMed / MEDLINE**, **Europe PMC**, **PMC** (full text), **Cochrane
  Library** (systematic reviews), **ClinicalTrials.gov** & **WHO ICTRP** (trial
  registration and outcome-switching checks), **Embase** (if available).
- Use **MeSH** terms and field tags in queries; combine with free-text synonyms.

## Evidence & design specifics
- Top of the hierarchy: **Cochrane/PRISMA systematic reviews & meta-analyses of
  RCTs**, then large well-conducted **RCTs**, then cohort → case-control →
  cross-sectional → case series/report → mechanistic/animal/in-vitro.
- Prefer **patient-relevant outcomes** (mortality, morbidity, quality of life)
  over **surrogate endpoints** (biomarkers). Flag when only surrogates exist.
- Check **absolute** effects (ARR, NNT/NNH), not just relative risk; note
  baseline risk. Distinguish efficacy (ideal) from effectiveness (real world).

## Appraisal tools (see `references/critical-appraisal-tools.md`)
- RCTs → **RoB 2**; non-randomised → **ROBINS-I**; observational → **Newcastle–
  Ottawa**; systematic reviews → **AMSTAR-2**; certainty → **GRADE**.
- Reporting guidelines to expect: **CONSORT** (RCTs), **PRISMA** (reviews),
  **STROBE** (observational), **STARD** (diagnostics), **PRISMA-DTA**.

## Red flags specific to the field
- **Trial registration mismatch / outcome switching** (compare paper to
  registry), selective reporting, industry sponsorship, ghost authorship.
- Over-reading **subgroup analyses**, composite endpoints, per-protocol vs.
  intention-to-treat, small single-centre "positive" trials not yet replicated.
- Predatory/OMICS-style journals; retractions (check per reproducibility pack).

## Consensus anchors
WHO, Cochrane, NICE, USPSTF, national academies, major specialty societies,
CDC/ECDC. Weight guideline recommendations by their own evidence grading.
